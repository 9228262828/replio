REPLIO PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Summary: REPLIO is an offline-first household refill tracker. The current core version does not require an account, Firebase, backend, cloud sync, ads, analytics, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or payment information.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how REPLIO, package com.replio.home, handles information in the current core version of the app.

2. Scope

This Policy applies to the REPLIO mobile application and these informational support pages.

3. Offline-First Design

REPLIO is designed as an offline-first household refill and stock tracking application. Its current core functionality does not require an internet connection.

4. No Account Required

You do not need to create a REPLIO account, username, password, or personal profile to use the current core version.

5. No Login Data

Because there is no login system in the current core version, REPLIO does not request sign-in credentials.

6. Household Item Data

You may enter household item names into REPLIO. These names are stored locally on your device.

7. Categories

You may assign items to built-in categories such as Kitchen, Bathroom, Cleaning, Food, Pets, Office, or Other.

8. Current Quantity

REPLIO may store the current quantity you enter for each tracked item.

9. Minimum Quantity

REPLIO may store the minimum quantity you choose for each tracked item.

10. Units

REPLIO may store units such as pieces, bottles, packs, kilograms, grams, liters, milliliters, boxes, or rolls.

11. Notes

You may enter optional notes for tracked items. Notes are stored locally on your device.

12. Favorite Status

REPLIO may store whether an item is marked as a favorite.

13. Last Refill Date

REPLIO may store the last refill date for a tracked item.

14. Refill History

When you use the Refilled action, REPLIO may store a local refill-history entry including the item name, quantity added, unit, and date.

15. Use-One Action

The quick minus-one action updates a tracked quantity locally on your device.

16. Smart Status

Status labels such as Good, Low, Refill Now, and Out are calculated locally from the quantity and minimum quantity values you enter.

17. Dashboard Counts

Counts such as items running low, items stocked, and items refilled this week are calculated locally.

18. Statistics

REPLIO may calculate local statistics such as total items, total refills, category counts, and most frequently refilled items.

19. Search

Search terms used to filter your local item list are processed on-device and are not sent to a REPLIO-operated server.

20. Category Filters

Category filtering is processed locally on your device.

21. Dark Mode Preference

Your dark-mode choice may be stored locally so REPLIO can remember your appearance preference.

22. Local Storage

The current core version uses local application storage, such as SharedPreferences, to store items, refill history, and preferences.

23. No Firebase

The current core version is not designed to use Firebase for authentication, databases, analytics, remote configuration, or cloud synchronization.

24. No Remote Backend

The current core version does not send your tracked household inventory to a REPLIO-operated backend.

25. No Cloud Sync

The current core version does not provide REPLIO-operated cloud synchronization between devices.

26. No Advertising

The current core version does not contain REPLIO-operated advertising.

27. No Personalized Ads

REPLIO does not use your locally stored household inventory data to deliver personalized advertising in the current core version.

28. No Behavioral Analytics

The current core version is not designed to collect behavioral analytics for profiling, marketing, or advertising.

29. No Cross-App Tracking

REPLIO is not designed to track your activity across unrelated third-party apps or websites.

30. No Sale of Personal Information

REPLIO does not intentionally sell or rent the locally stored information described in this Policy.

31. No Precise Location

The current core version does not require precise or approximate device location permission.

32. No Camera

The current core version does not require camera access.

33. No Microphone

The current core version does not require microphone access or audio recording.

34. No Contacts

The current core version does not require access to your contacts.

35. No Phone or SMS

The current core version does not require phone-call, call-log, or SMS permissions.

36. No Calendar

The current core version does not require access to your device calendar.

37. No Photos or Media

The current core version does not require access to your photo library, gallery, videos, or media library.

38. No General File Access

The current core version does not require access to your personal documents or general file storage for normal use.

39. No Payment Information

The current core version does not require payment-card information, bank credentials, or payment account details.

40. No Purchases in Core Version

The current core version described by this Policy does not require an in-app purchase to use its core functionality.

41. No Social Features

The current core version does not include public profiles, social feeds, direct messaging, friend lists, or user-to-user chat.

42. No User-Generated Public Content

The current core version does not publish your item names, notes, quantities, or refill history to other users.

43. No Sensitive Data Requirement

REPLIO does not require health information, government identifiers, passwords, payment credentials, or other highly sensitive personal information for normal use.

44. Personal Notes

Because notes are free text, avoid entering passwords, payment credentials, government identifiers, medical secrets, or other highly sensitive information.

45. Data Retention

Saved items and refill history generally remain on your device until you delete them, reset all app data, clear application storage, uninstall the app, or the operating system removes the data.

46. Delete Individual Items

You may delete individual tracked items from the app.

47. Delete History Entries

You may remove individual refill-history entries from the app.

48. Reset All Data

REPLIO includes a reset option that can remove all tracked items and refill history from the app's local storage.

49. Uninstalling the App

Uninstalling REPLIO may remove locally stored information, subject to operating-system or device backup and restore behavior.

50. Device Backups

Your operating system or device provider may back up application data according to your device settings. Such backups are not controlled by REPLIO's core app logic.

51. Data Recovery

Because the current core version does not include REPLIO-operated cloud backup, we may not be able to recover deleted items or refill history.

52. Device Security

You are responsible for protecting access to your device using available security features such as a passcode, PIN, password, or biometric lock.

53. Children

REPLIO is a general household organization tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children through accounts, messaging, public profiles, or online social features.

54. Third-Party Platforms

App stores and device platforms may independently process account, device, installation, security, purchase, or diagnostic information under their own privacy policies.

55. Google Play

If you obtain REPLIO through Google Play, Google may process installation, account, device, security, purchase, or diagnostic information under Google's own policies and terms.

56. GitHub Pages

These informational pages may be hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web-server information according to its own policies. These pages do not require you to submit REPLIO household inventory data.

57. Support Communications

If you contact us at alihisny123@gmail.com, your email provider and our email provider will process the information you voluntarily include in your message.

58. Support Safety

Do not send passwords, payment credentials, private keys, government identifiers, or other highly sensitive information in support emails.

59. Legal Requirements

Information voluntarily provided through support communications may be disclosed if required to comply with applicable law or valid legal process.

60. International Use

You may use REPLIO in different countries. Local app data remains on your device in the current core version, while app stores, email providers, and website hosts may process their own data in other jurisdictions.

61. Security

We aim to minimize data handling by keeping current core functionality local. No method of device storage or electronic processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

62. Accuracy of Inventory Data

REPLIO stores and displays the quantities you enter. It cannot independently verify whether your real-world household stock matches the values recorded in the app.

63. No Professional Inventory Guarantee

REPLIO is intended for personal household organization and is not a guaranteed professional inventory-management system.

64. Changes to the App

The app may receive updates that improve organization features, design, compatibility, accessibility, or stability.

65. Future Online Features

If future versions add accounts, cloud sync, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, purchases, online sharing, or other online features, this Policy should be reviewed and updated before release.

66. Future Permissions

If a future feature requires a new device permission, REPLIO should request it only when needed for that feature and the Privacy Policy should be updated accordingly.

67. Your Choices

You may edit or delete items, delete history entries, reset all local data, change app preferences, clear app storage, uninstall REPLIO, or stop using the app.

68. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when REPLIO changes or when legal, technical, distribution, or app-store requirements change.

69. Effective Date

This Privacy Policy is effective as of October 9, 2026.

70. Contact
